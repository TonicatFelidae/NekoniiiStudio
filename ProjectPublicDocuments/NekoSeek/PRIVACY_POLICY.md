# Privacy Policy for NekoSeek

**Last updated:** October 13, 2026

Nekoniii Studio ("we", "our", or "us") develops and operates the mobile application **NekoSeek** (the "Application") for Android devices, available on Google Play.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our Application.

---

## 1. Information Collection and Use

### Personal Information Collected by Nekoniii Studio
We do not directly collect, store, sell, or share any personally identifiable information (such as your name, physical address, email address, or phone number). The core gameplay runs locally on your device.

### Third-Party Services and Advertising (Device or other IDs)
Although we do not collect personal data ourselves, the Application may integrate third-party services and Advertising SDKs (such as Google AdMob, Google Play Services, or Unity Ads) to serve in-game advertisements and support the free availability of the game.

These third-party service providers may automatically collect and process certain information, specifically:
- **Device or other IDs**: Unique identifiers such as Google Advertising ID (AAID), Android ID, device model, hardware specifications, and network carrier.
- **Analytics & Diagnostic Data**: Information regarding ad impressions, clicks, app interactions, crash reports, and performance diagnostics.

### Why This Data Is Collected
Third-party advertising and analytics partners collect **Device or other IDs** for the following purposes:
1. **Advertising & Marketing**: Delivering relevant ads, controlling ad frequency (frequency capping), and measuring ad campaign effectiveness.
2. **Analytics**: Understanding performance, app stability, and usage patterns to optimize the application experience.
3. **Fraud Prevention, Security, and Compliance**: Detecting invalid clicks, combating fraudulent activity, and ensuring system security.

## 3. Managing and Opting Out of Targeted Advertising

You have control over how your device's advertising identifier is used:
- On your Android device, go to **Settings** > **Privacy** > **Ads** (or **Settings** > **Google** > **Ads**).
- You can choose to **Reset advertising ID** or **Delete advertising ID**, or opt out of personalized advertising.

---

## 4. Children's Privacy

The Application does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate measures.

## 5. Security

We value your trust in using our Application. All communications between the app's integrated third-party SDKs and their servers use industry-standard encryption protocols (HTTPS/SSL) in transit.
